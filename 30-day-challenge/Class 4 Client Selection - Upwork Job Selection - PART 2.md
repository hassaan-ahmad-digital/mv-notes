> Search Setup
    - keyword "video edit" search kro, kabhi "editor/editing" nahi. root word sari variations pakar leta h. Apni searches save krlo.
    - Target first world countries only, otherwise feed will double, achi jobs utni hi rehti hain.
    - Rates: hourly min $10, max infinity. Fixed price bina range k include kro (amounts placeholders hote hain)
    - 10+ hires filter: total spent $3000 se kam = $300/freelancer se kam. Esi job kholo hi mat
    - Reload tactic: 1-5 min purani job pakro. Wo client abhi computer pr betha h or foran hire kr sakta h
    - Boost heavy: seedha 30 connects. 15 connects boost sham tak outbid ho jata h or aap doob jate ho
    - Har bid track kro: job-link, connects used, bid speed, sab connect tracking sheet main add kro. NO DATA = NO LEARNING
    - VCL (video cover letter) will give you unfair advantage

> The 6 step job analysis
    Sabse sastay signals pehle, description sabse akhir main. Ta k bura client kabhi ek nazar se ziada cost na kre. 1-2 chote red flags pr kabhi reject na kro. Red flag ka stack = verdict.
    
    Client rejection ka alag se journal kro k mene isko reject q kia tha, or in future check kro k kya sahi reject kia tha ya ghalat reject kia tha? 

    Step 1: Feed filter (bina click kiye)
        - Posted 1-5 min pehle | premium location | $3k/$300 rule pass | proposals 5 se kam.
    Step 2: About the client sidebar (description nahi)
        - Hire rate 60% se uper (niche = window shopper, jese sab ko ghost kiya wese aap ko bhi kre ga)
        - total spent per hire $300 se uper. Average hourly paid $10 se uper
        - Rating: new account pr 0 theek he but history ke sath agar 2-4 stars hain to bhag jao. Rating k piche review count zaroor check kro
    Step 3: Reviews aur history
        - Chahiay: Mahinon lambay contracts walay, hazaron dollars billed. Avoid: lambe contracts choti totals k sath, bar bar repeat
        - kis ko hire krte hain: sirf $3/hr asians = cheep hunter. Sirf natives = English profile chahiay. Mix countries fair rates pr = darwaza khula 
        - Review text parho: "micromanager", "poor communication". Gussay walay 1 star = aapka future treatment
        - Reviews se clients ka first name utha lo, cover letter + google/linkedin search k liay
    Step 4: Is job pr activity
        - 10+ invites bheje = aap ka proposal dafan.
        - Last viewed 3+ days pehle = client ja chuka h ya DM main negotiate kr raha h
        - Green: Invites unanswered, zero interviews = darwaza pura khula, abhi bid kro
    Step 5: Preferred qualification,
        - Preferred location PH/PK/IN = cheap hunt. US/UK = acha client, hum imandari se bid krte hain, ye jaante huay k mismatch nazar aye ga
        - 90%+ JSS mangta h = serious buyer he, 90% se niche target krta h = saste desperate freelancers dhoond raha h
    Step 6: Description parho (sabse akhir main)
        - Pehle end scan kro $ numbers k liay: asli budget akhri lines main chupa hota h
        - Cash cow flags: 8-12 minute videos, AI voiceover/11 labs, stock footage, named hobby channels. Hamesha fatal nahi, acha budget ho to bid worth it ho sakti h
        - Commanding tone: ("Do not apply if...") = toxic revisions aur JSS risk
        - Contract se pehle off platform push = Upwork pr likh k refuse kro. Contract k baad bahar chat OK, payment upwork pr hi rahegi.
        - Mismatches: expert kam entry budget pr, 5hr/week vs boht bara scope. Platform illitracy ho sakti h, pooch lo.
        - Currency check: CAD 10,000 asal me ~= $8,000. Poocho kabhi assume mat kro.

> Green vs Red flags (Quick Reference)

        | Signal | Green | Red |
        |---|---|---|
        | Hire Rate | 60%+, ideally 80%+ | 60% se niche, ghost krega |
        | Spent vs hires | $300+ avg per freelancer | $300 se kam avg per freelancer |
        | Avg hourly paid | $10+, ideally $25 | $2-3 history |
        | Rating | Boht reviews pr 4.9+ | History k sath 2-4 stars |
        | Review Text | Ache alfaz, repeat hires | Micromanager, poor communication, gussay walay 1 star |
        | Hiring Pattern | mixed countries, fair rates, lambe contracts | sirf sasta, lambay contracts, choti total |
        | Title Keywords | Clear need, asli business | LONG TERM caps, cash cow, YT automation, hobby channels |
        | Budget | Realistic, scope se match | Uper bara placeholder, neechay $20/video |
        | Duration | 6+ months OR 30+ hours per week | One-offs, 5hr/week with bara scope |
        | Activity | Fresh, 5 se kam proposals, no interviews | 10+ invites, interviews chal rahe, 3+ days se view nahi |
        | Location | US, UK, AU, DE | 3rd world; canada per alert |
        | Tone | Clear, Respectful | Hukum, dhamkiyan, over-demanding |
        | Off-platform | Contract aur payment upwork pr | contract se pehle whatsapp/telegram push |
        | JSS | 90% plus mangta h | 90% se kam target krta h |

> Bidding Execution
    - Reuseable Video Cover Letters (VCL): har job type k liay ek master video. Unki job post kabhi mat dikhao, apni screen, portfolio results dikhao. Ek video 100s of bids.
    - Screening question templates: repeat hone wale sawalon k ready answers; complex sawalon k liay ek pre recorded loom, link paste kro.
    - Job k skills tag apni profile pr mirror kro. Clients "X jobs done in this category" dekhte hain; zero = aap unke liay ajnabi ho.
    - Har bid submit hote hi connect tracking sheet main log kro.

> 7 laws
    - Sakht process = maximum outreach, maximum sales, minimum time
    - Trust payment k sath aata h
    - Mentality pese se ziada important h
    - Ek red flag caution h, pattern verdict h
    - Margins aap ka treatment decide krte hain: High margin client k liay aap ROI ho, low margin k liay ek cost.
    - Sab kuch track kro: Data hi system or aadat k beech ka farq h.
    - Specialize krte raho jab tak ek premium nich aap ki apni na ho jae.
